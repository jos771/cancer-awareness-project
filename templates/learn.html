Understanding Cancer

Simple information to help you understand the basics of cancer.

What is cancer?

Cancer is a group of diseases involving abnormal cells that can grow and divide in an uncontrolled way. Different cancers can affect different parts of the body.

Why does awareness matter?

Cancer awareness helps people understand health information, recognise changes that may need attention, and know when to seek advice from healthcare professionals.

Different types of cancer

There are many types of cancer. Examples include breast cancer, prostate cancer, lung cancer, cervical cancer and testicular cancer.

Each type can have different risk factors, symptoms and treatment approaches.

Can cancer be prevented?

Not all cancers can be prevented. However, some healthy behaviours and reducing certain risk factors can lower the risk of developing some cancers.

Regular healthcare check-ups and recommended screening can also help with early detection of some cancers.

Remember

Having a particular change or symptom does not automatically mean someone has cancer. Only a qualified healthcare professional can properly assess a health concern.
