Myths & Facts

Good health decisions start with reliable information.

MYTH

Every lump means cancer.

FACT

Many lumps and swellings have causes other than cancer. Persistent or concerning changes should be assessed by a healthcare professional.

MYTH

If something doesn't hurt, it cannot be serious.

FACT

Some important health changes may not cause pain. That is why new or persistent changes should not automatically be ignored.

MYTH

Self-checking can diagnose cancer.

FACT

Self-awareness can help you notice changes, but only appropriate medical assessment can determine what is causing a change.

MYTH

Cancer always has obvious symptoms.

FACT

Some cancers can have few or no obvious symptoms early on. Screening recommendations and medical advice depend on the person's circumstances.
