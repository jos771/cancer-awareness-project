Testicular Cancer Awareness

Understand changes that may need professional medical attention.

🩺

Know what is normal for you

Become familiar with your normal size, shape and feel so that unusual persistent changes are easier to notice.

Changes to be aware of

  • A new or unusual lump.
  • Persistent swelling.
  • A change in size or shape.
  • Another persistent unusual change.

Have a concern?

A change does not automatically mean cancer. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional for proper assessment.
