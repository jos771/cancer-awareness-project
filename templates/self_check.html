THE CAP APPROACH

Look. Notice. Understand. Act.

CAP is designed to help you become familiar with your body. It is not designed to diagnose cancer.

01

Look

Notice changes in appearance that are unusual for you.

02

Notice

Become familiar with what normally feels and looks normal for you.

03

Understand

Learn which changes should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

04

Act

If you are concerned about a persistent or unusual change, seek professional advice.

⚠️

Important

Finding a change does not mean that you have cancer. Many changes have other causes. CAP cannot diagnose cancer. A qualified healthcare professional should assess concerns.
