BE AWARE

Warning Signs

Some unusual or persistent changes should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

01

A new lump or swelling

A new or persistent lump or swelling anywhere on the body should be assessed when it does not settle or is concerning.

02

Unexplained bleeding

Unusual or unexplained bleeding should be discussed with a healthcare professional.

03

Persistent changes

A change that does not go away, continues to grow, or keeps changing deserves professional assessment.

04

Unusual skin changes

New or changing moles, marks or persistent skin changes should be checked when concerning.

05

Persistent mouth changes

Mouth sores, patches, lumps or other unusual changes that persist should be professionally assessed.

06

Unexplained changes

Persistent unexplained changes in your health should not simply be ignored.

💡

Awareness does not mean panic

These signs can have many causes that are not cancer. Their purpose is to help people recognise when it may be appropriate to seek professional advice.
