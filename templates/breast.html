Breast Cancer Awareness

Learn about breast health and changes that may need professional attention.

🎗️

Know what is normal for you

Becoming familiar with how your breasts normally look and feel can make it easier to notice a new or unusual change.

Changes to be aware of

  • A new or unusual lump or swelling.
  • Changes in appearance.
  • Changes around the nipple.
  • Unusual discharge.
  • A persistent change that is not normal for you.

Something concerns you?

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional for proper assessment.
